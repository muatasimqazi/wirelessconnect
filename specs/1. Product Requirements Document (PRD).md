# 1. Executive Summary

Wireless Connect currently operates a physical retail and repair business specializing in:

- Used smartphones
- Device repair
- Accessories
- Laptops and tablets

The goal is to launch a modern e-commerce platform that enables customers to:

- Browse inventory online
- Purchase devices with nationwide U.S. shipping
- Schedule local pickup at the Shoreline, WA store
- Submit trade-ins (Phase 2)
- Book repairs (Phase 2)

The system should be built with minimal recurring cost and allow complete ownership of data and customer relationships.

The primary target customers are value-conscious consumers seeking reliable used devices. Wireless Connect serves:

- Local customers in Shoreline, North Seattle, Lynnwood, Edmonds, Mountlake Terrace, and surrounding areas
- Customers anywhere in the continental United States (shipping)
- Multilingual communities including Spanish-speaking customers

In the future, Wireless Connect may expand to serve international customers globally.

- Coupon validated atomically before Stripe session creation
- Customer locale stored on order at checkout

# 12. Orders

## Status Flow

### Pickup Orders

```text
Pending
→ Paid
→ Processing
→ Ready for Pickup
→ Picked Up
```

### Shipping Orders

```text
Pending
→ Paid
→ Processing
→ Shipped (with tracking number + carrier)
→ Delivered
```

### Cancelled Orders

```text
Paid → Cancelled (only before processing begins)
```

## Guest Order Access

Guest customers can view their order confirmation via:

- Order confirmation URL with `guest_access_token`
- Order lookup by email + order number (for support)

Order lookup must validate `guest_access_token` — never expose orders by order number or order ID alone.

## Customer Cancellation

- Customers may request cancellation of orders in `Paid` or `Processing` status.
- Cancellation is not guaranteed — admin must review and approve.
- Cancellation requests are persisted for staff review; request submission does not automatically cancel or refund the order.
- If approved, a cancellation confirmation email is sent.
- Refund is initiated separately via Stripe dashboard or admin action.

# 14. Warranty System

## Warranty Record Creation

- Warranty records are created automatically after successful payment for eligible devices.
- Warranty duration is taken from the order item snapshot, not from the current product record.

## Customer Warranty Claim Submission

Customers can submit a warranty claim by:

1. Logging in and navigating to their warranty in Account → Warranty.
2. Describing the issue.
3. Submitting the claim.

Claim status:

- `none` — no claim submitted
- `submitted` — claim received
- `under_review` — staff reviewing
- `approved` — claim approved
- `denied` — claim denied
- `resolved` — claim resolved

Staff/admin can update claim status and add internal notes via the Warranties module in the admin dashboard.

## Warranty Email Notifications

- Customer receives email confirmation when claim is submitted.
- Customer receives email when claim status changes.

# 19. Legal Pages

The following legal pages are required before launch. They are customer-facing, translated into English and Spanish, and accessible from the footer.

## Terms of Service

- Covers: acceptable use, returns, warranty terms, dispute resolution, limitation of liability.
- Include: used device disclaimer, condition representation, no guarantee of carrier unlock status at time of future carrier policy changes.

## Privacy Policy

- Covers: what data is collected, how it is used, who it is shared with.
- Must include: CCPA-specific language for California residents.
- Must include: "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" section.
- Must include: data deletion request process (45-day response time for CCPA).

## Return & Exchange Policy

- Wireless Connect offers a 30-day limited warranty (or as specified per product).
- Returns accepted within the warranty period for covered defects.
- No returns for buyer's remorse unless required by applicable state law.
- Process: contact store, describe issue, ship back or bring in-store, staff inspects, approve or deny.
- Refunds issued to original payment method via Stripe.
- Clarify: the "no returns" rule in the in-store Used Device Purchase Policy applies to the **store purchasing from sellers** — not to customer returns.

## Warranty Policy

- Duration: 30 days standard (device-specific).
- Coverage: manufacturing defects, hardware failures not caused by physical damage, liquid damage, or unauthorized modification.
- Exclusions: physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repair, software issues, accessories.
- Claim process: submit via account or contact store.
- Resolution: repair, replacement, or refund at Wireless Connect's discretion.

## Shipping Policy

- Nationwide U.S. shipping.
- Shipping carrier options (USPS, UPS, FedEx).
- Estimated delivery times (3–7 business days standard).
- Shipping insurance (included for orders over $200).
- Signature confirmation (for orders over $500).
- Lithium battery shipping compliance note.
- No international shipping (current).
- Lost/damaged shipment process.

# 25. Compliance Requirements

## Washington State — Secondhand Dealer (RCW 19.60)

- Record seller identity (government ID) for all purchased devices
- Record IMEI and serial number
- Hold devices for minimum 5 days before resale
- Make records available to law enforcement upon request
- Staff must be trained on this requirement

## Multi-State Sales Tax (U.S. Nationwide)

- Use Stripe Tax for automatic calculation in all states
- Monitor economic nexus thresholds (most states: $100K or 200 transactions/year)
- Maintain Stripe Tax transaction records for state filing

## CCPA (California Customers)

- Privacy Policy includes CCPA language
- "Do Not Sell" disclosure in footer
- Data deletion requests processed within 45 days

## International (Future)

- OFAC sanctions screening before enabling international checkout
- Carrier-specific lithium battery shipping compliance (IATA for air freight)
- Country-specific import duties disclosed to customer at checkout
