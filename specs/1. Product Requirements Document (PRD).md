# 1. Executive Summary

Wireless Connect currently operates a physical retail and repair business specializing in:

- Used smartphones
- Device repair
- Accessories
- Laptops and tablets

The goal is to launch a modern e-commerce platform that enables customers to:

- Browse inventory online
- Purchase devices
- Schedule local pickup
- Request shipping
- Submit trade-ins
- Book repairs

The system should be built with minimal recurring cost and allow complete ownership of data and customer relationships.

The primary target customers are value-conscious consumers seeking reliable used devices, including immigrants and multilingual communities throughout Shoreline, North Seattle, Lynnwood, Edmonds, Mountlake Terrace, and surrounding areas.
