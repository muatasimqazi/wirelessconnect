# 1. Inventory Intake Workflow

Defines how a device enters inventory and becomes available for sale.

## Workflow

```text
Acquire Device
→ Inspect Device
→ Verify IMEI
→ Test Device
→ Grade Condition
→ Photograph Device
→ Create Listing
→ Publish Product
```

## Required Intake Checklist

Every device must have:

- Brand
- Model
- Storage
- Color
- Carrier
- IMEI
- Serial Number
- Battery Health
- Physical Condition
- Functional Test Result

# 2. Device Testing Standards

Every device must be tested before it can be listed.

## Required Functional Tests

- Power On
- Touchscreen
- Face ID / Touch ID
- Cameras
- Speakers
- Microphone
- Charging Port
- Wireless Charging
- Bluetooth
- WiFi
- Cellular Connectivity
- Volume Buttons
- Power Button
- Battery Health

## Listing Rule

Any failed test must be disclosed on the product page.

## Testing Documentation

For every device:

- Record tester
- Record test date
- Record testing notes
- Store evidence photos when necessary

Testing records should be retained for warranty support and quality control.

# 3. Condition Grading Standards

Used devices must follow a consistent grading system.

## Like New

Requirements:

- No visible scratches
- No dents
- No cracks
- Battery Health ≥ 95%

## Excellent

Requirements:

- Minor signs of use
- No cracks
- No dents
- Battery Health ≥ 90%

## Good

Requirements:

- Visible scratches
- No functional issues
- Battery Health ≥ 85%

## Fair

Requirements:

- Heavy cosmetic wear
- Fully functional
- Battery Health ≥ 80%

## Below Fair

Requirements:

- Major functional issues
- Battery Health < 80%

Policy:

- Do not list online
- Sell locally only
- Clearly disclose issues

# 6. Photography Standards

All devices should use the same photo sequence.

## Required Photos

### Image 1

Front View

### Image 2

Back View

### Image 3

Left Side

### Image 4

Right Side

### Image 5

Top

### Image 6

Bottom

### Image 7

Battery Health Screen

### Image 8

Imperfections / Cosmetic Wear

Required if defects exist.

## Internal Verification Photos

May include:

- Battery health screen
- IMEI verification evidence
- Activation-ready verification
- Cosmetic damage documentation
- Testing evidence photos

These photos may be stored internally and are not required to appear on customer-facing product pages.

Internal verification photos should never expose customer information.

## Photo Background

Approved:

- White
- Light Gray

Not Allowed:

- Store counters
- Cluttered backgrounds
- Hands/fingers visible
- Watermarks

## Localization Requirements

### Image Alt Text

Every product image should support localized alt text.

Example:

English:

```text
Front view of Apple iPhone 15 Pro Max
```

Spanish:

```text
Vista frontal del Apple iPhone 15 Pro Max
```

### No Text in Images

Avoid placing important product information directly inside images.

Information that must remain translatable should be stored as website content instead of image text.

Examples:

- Warranty information
- Condition descriptions
- Battery health explanations
- Shipping information
- Promotional messaging

## Multilingual Product Content Standards

Customer-facing product content should support English and Spanish for MVP.

Translate:

- Product descriptions
- SEO titles
- SEO descriptions
- Warranty explanations
- Condition explanations
- Battery health helper text
- Shipping/pickup explanations
- Product image alt text
- Trust badge explanations
- Product condition helper text
- Warranty helper text
- Homepage marketing content

Do not translate:

- Brand names
- Model names
- Storage values
- Color names unless a standard localized version is available
- SKU
- IMEI
- Serial number

## Localized Product Fields

English and Spanish should be supported for:

- Product Description
- SEO Title
- SEO Description
- Image Alt Text
- Warranty Description
- Condition Explanation
- Trust Badge Explanations
- Homepage Marketing Content
- Category Descriptions

### Plain-Language Requirements

Customer-facing content should use simple language.

Avoid technical jargon when possible.

If technical terminology is necessary, provide a short explanation.

Examples:

Unlocked:

```text
Works with most carriers.
```

Battery Health:

```text
Higher battery health means longer battery life.
```

Clean IMEI:

```text
Verified and ready to activate.
```

### Localized Product Content Requirements

The following content should support localization:

- Product descriptions
- Condition descriptions
- Warranty descriptions
- Shipping information
- Pickup information
- Product image alt text
- SEO titles
- SEO descriptions
- Trust badge explanations
- Homepage hero content
- Category descriptions
- Search empty-state messages
- Marketing banners

The following content may remain unchanged across languages:

- Brand names
- Model names
- SKU values
- IMEI values
- Serial numbers
- Storage capacities

# 16. Inventory Performance KPIs

These metrics should eventually appear in the Admin Dashboard.

## Active Inventory Count

```text
Total Active Products
```

## Average Days in Inventory

Formula:

```text
Published Date → Sold Date
```

## Sell-Through Rate

Formula:

```text
Sold Devices ÷ Total Devices
```

## Inventory Value

Formula:

```text
Sum(Cost)
```

Admin only.

## Inventory Aging Rules

### 30+ Days

- Review pricing

### 60+ Days

- Consider promotion

### 90+ Days

- Consider clearance pricing

### 120+ Days

- Review for archival or local-sale strategy

# 19. MVP Publishing Requirements

Before a product can be published:

- Clean IMEI verified
- Fully tested
- Factory reset completed
- Activation Lock removed
- Minimum 6 photos uploaded
- Condition assigned
- Price assigned
- Warranty assigned
- Quantity assigned

If any requirement is missing:

```text
Product cannot be published.
```

The system should block publishing and display validation errors.

# 21. Acceptance Criteria

The Content & Inventory Strategy is considered implemented when:

1. Every published product follows naming standards.
2. Every published phone has passed the testing checklist.
3. Every published phone has a condition grade.
4. Every published phone has battery health recorded.
5. Every published phone has a clean IMEI verification.
6. Every published phone has at least 6 images.
7. Every published product includes warranty information.
8. Product images follow naming standards.
9. Featured products follow featured-product rules.
10. Inventory KPIs can be calculated from stored data.
11. Internal intake statuses support device preparation workflows.
12. Internal verification photos can be stored separately from customer-facing product images.
13. Devices include tester, testing date, and testing notes.
14. Data wipe verification is required before publishing.
15. Warranty claim tracking supports support workflows.
16. WhatsApp support links are available where configured.
17. Products cannot be published when required fields are missing.
18. The inventory structure supports future supplier imports and trade-ins.
19. Product descriptions support English and Spanish.
20. Product image alt text supports localization.
21. Important information is not embedded in product images.
22. SEO titles and descriptions support localization.
23. Customer-facing product content follows plain-language guidelines.
24. Product content architecture supports future multilingual expansion.
25. Product descriptions, SEO titles, and SEO descriptions are localization-ready.
26. Customer-facing marketing content is translatable.
