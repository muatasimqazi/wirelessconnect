# 1. Purpose

This document defines the multilingual, localization, and accessibility strategy for the Wireless Connect Online Store.

The goal is to make the website usable by customers with limited English proficiency while maintaining a simple and trustworthy shopping experience.

This strategy is especially important because Wireless Connect serves a diverse customer base in:

- Shoreline, WA
- North Seattle
- Lynnwood
- Mountlake Terrace
- Edmonds
- South Snohomish County

Many customers may prefer shopping in languages other than English.

---

# 2. Business Goals

## Primary Goals

# 5. Localization Principles

## Principles

1. Never hardcode customer-facing text.
2. Every customer-facing string must be translatable.
3. Use plain language.
4. Use icons where helpful.
5. Avoid technical jargon.
6. Use translation keys instead of inline text.
7. Keep English as the source language.

# 10. Language Switcher

## Requirement

Provide language switching from every page.

---

## Desktop

Location:

```text
Top-right corner of header
```

Display:

```text
English | Español
```

---

## Mobile

Location:

```text
Navigation drawer
```

---

## Behavior

Switching language should:

- Preserve current page
- Preserve route
- Preserve cart
- Preserve session

Example:

```text
/en/products/iphone-15
```

becomes

```text
/es/products/iphone-15
```

# 13. Accessibility Through Language

Many customers may not understand technical phone terminology.

The website should provide plain-language explanations.

---

## Unlocked

Display helper text:

```text
Works with most carriers.
```

```text
Funciona con la mayoría de las compañías telefónicas.
```

---

## Battery Health

Display helper text:

```text
Higher battery health means longer battery life.
```

Spanish:

```text
Una mayor salud de la batería significa una mayor duración.
```

---

## IMEI

Display helper text:

```text
Unique phone identifier used to verify activation eligibility.
```

Spanish:

```text
Identificador único utilizado para verificar la activación del teléfono.
```

# 21. Acceptance Criteria

Localization strategy is complete when:

1. English and Spanish are supported.
2. Customer-facing text is translatable.
3. Locale-aware routing exists.
4. Language switcher works globally.
5. Customer locale is persisted.
6. Emails support localization.
7. Technical phone terminology includes explanations.
8. WhatsApp contact options are available.
9. SEO supports multiple languages.
10. The architecture supports future languages without major refactoring.
11. Mobile and desktop experiences are fully localized.
12. English remains the fallback language.
